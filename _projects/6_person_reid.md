**Person Re-ID** implements a mixed precision approach for real-time person re-identification on edge devices.

## Key Features

- ResNet-50 and MobileNetV2 backbone support
- NVIDIA Apex half-precision (FP16/FP32) inference
- Real-time tracking on embedded GPUs
- Trained on DukeMTC, CUHK03, and Market1501 datasets
- Component of the REVAMP2T surveillance pipeline

## Publication

M. Baharani, S. Mohan, and H. Tabkhi, "Real-Time Person Re-identification at the Edge," *ICIAR 2019*. (17 citations)
